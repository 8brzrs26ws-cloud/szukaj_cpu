## szukaj_cpu 1.0.4

### Web panel `/admin/`
* Explanation for new users: what the **zone** (where a person must be to raise an alarm)
  and the **crop** (which part of the frame the model sees) do, with illustrations and
  advice on which one to use. The section can be collapsed (remembered in the browser).
* Live summary under the image: what the current settings will do, a warning that people
  outside the crop are not detected, and the **magnification** the crop gives – a strip as
  wide as the frame does not enlarge people (×1.0), with a hint to use the zone instead.
